Frequently asked questions

How can your fares be lower?

Consolidator fares are negotiated in volume and are not always shown on public booking sites. Savings vary by route, date and availability.

Are these genuine airline tickets?

Yes. Tickets are issued for the airline you choose, and your quote lists the fare rules before you commit.

Can I change or cancel a booking?

It depends on the fare. Your advisor explains change and refund conditions in your quote so you can choose with confidence.

How quickly will I get a quote?

Send your route and dates through the contact form. An advisor will reply with available fares as soon as possible.

Do you book for assistants and families?

Yes. We can book for executive assistants, partners, family and groups, and keep every detail private.

Privacy Policy

What we collect, why we collect it, and the terms that apply when you send us a quote request.

By sending your request you agree to this Privacy Policy.

1. Nature of the quote request

Submitting a request is not an offer, does not oblige Fly First Class International to provide any service, and does not create a binding contract. You agree to provide accurate, complete and current information, including names as they appear on travel documents, routes, dates and passenger numbers. Any quote or estimate based on inaccurate or incomplete information is void.

2. Validity and expiration

A quote is valid for 14 days from the date it is issued, subject to fare availability, unless it states otherwise. We may amend, withdraw or cancel a quote at any time before you formally accept it.

3. Pricing and scope adjustments

A quote is an estimate based on the information you provide and current fares, not a fixed price. Fares are not guaranteed until tickets are issued. Any change to your itinerary, dates, cabin, passengers or other requirements after your request will result in a revised quote. We are not bound by obvious pricing errors, typographical mistakes or technical glitches on this website or in any quote, and may correct them before acceptance.

4. Intellectual property and confidentiality

Information and documents you submit with your request are licensed to us solely to review your request and prepare your quote. Our quote documentation, including fare construction and routing strategies, is confidential and must not be shared with competitors or other third parties without our written consent.

5. Communication and marketing consent

By submitting a request you agree that we may contact you by email, phone or message about your quote. You can opt out of these messages at any time. If you tick the newsletter box we will also send you marketing emails, and you can unsubscribe at any time using the link in each email. We handle personal information as described in this Privacy Policy and comply with applicable anti-spam and data protection laws, including CAN-SPAM and the GDPR.

Each request is given a reference ID. We keep the request, your name, email and phone number with that ID for up to six months from its last change so you can look it up, change it and reuse your details. Anyone who has the ID can view and change the request, so keep it private.

6. Data we collect, cookies and your consent

This section lists everything this site collects and why.

Details you give us. When you send a request we collect what you type into the form: your name, email, phone number, trip details, message and newsletter choice. We email it to our team and keep it under your reference ID for up to six months from its last change, so you can look it up, change it and reuse your details. We also record the time you sent it.

Cookies and similar browser storage (essential, always on). This site collects cookies and similar browser storage (such as local and session storage) that it needs to work: your light or dark mode choice, your cookie consent choice, the trip summary you are building during your visit, and, only if our server cannot be reached, requests saved in your browser. These stay on your device and are not sent to us. The site also looks up your approximate area from your IP address, using a location service, to suggest nearby airports. We do not store that result.

Optional data, only if you press Accept in the Consent to Cookies box. When you send a request, we add the following to the email our team receives:

  • your approximate location (city, region and country) worked out from your IP address, and the IP address itself;
  • your browser and its version, operating system, language and time zone;
  • your device hardware: number of processor cores, approximate memory, screen size and pixel density, window size and touch support;
  • your browser's user agent text.

These details are used only to help our team verify and serve your request and to protect against misuse. They are not stored on the site, not used for advertising and not sold. If you press Decline, none of these details are collected. The time the request was sent is always included.

Third parties. Photos, fonts and airline logos are loaded from other services (for example Unsplash, Google Fonts and airline logo providers), which receive your IP address and browser details when your browser fetches them. Our email provider delivers requests to us.

Changing your mind. Use Cookie settings at the bottom of any page to reopen the Consent to Cookies box and change your choice at any time. To have a request deleted before six months, email us with your reference ID.
