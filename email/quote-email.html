Good to know

 

Seat availability

Because these fares are priced well below the market, seats are limited and your exact dates may not always be open. A little flexibility greatly improves the chance of securing a discounted seat.

If we cannot confirm your preferred dates straight away, we can reserve dates close to them. In most cases seats are released later, often one to two weeks before departure, and your ticket can then be moved across. Tickets can be changed, subject to seat availability and the conditions in our Terms of Service.

 

Last-minute tickets

Travelling at short notice? There is a very good chance we can secure a discounted last-minute ticket. When a flight is not full, airlines usually release their remaining seats three to seven days before departure.
