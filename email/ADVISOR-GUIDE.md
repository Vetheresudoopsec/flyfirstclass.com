# Quote email guide for advisors

## What this is

The quote email is a ready-made layout for sending clients their ticket options. You add a screenshot and price for each option, fill in a few details and send. The booking request, the "Good to know" notes and the link to our Terms of Service are already in it, so nobody retypes them.

## Set it up once

Save the template in your mailbox one time, then reuse it for every quote. You need the file `quote-email.html`; ask the office for a copy.

### Copy the layout

1. Double-click `quote-email.html` so it opens in Chrome, or in Safari on a Mac.
2. Select everything (Ctrl+A on Windows, Cmd+A on Mac) and copy it (Ctrl+C or Cmd+C).

The page itself cannot be edited. You fill it in after pasting it into an email.

### Gmail

1. Turn templates on once: Settings (gear) > See all settings > Advanced > Templates > Enable > Save changes.
2. Start a new message and paste.
3. Click the three dots at the bottom right of the message > Templates > Save draft as template > Save as new template. Name it "Client quote".
4. Delete the draft with the trash icon. The template stays saved.
5. For each quote, start a new message and choose three dots > Templates > Client quote.

### Apple Mail

Apple Mail has no template feature for formatted emails, so you keep a blank copy in Drafts.

1. Start a new message and paste.
2. Close the window and choose Save. Never fill in this draft.
3. For each quote, open the draft, select all and copy, then paste into a new message. Fill in the new message only.

### Outlook

Outlook works like Apple Mail: paste into a new message, save it as a draft, and copy from the draft for each quote. We have not tested Outlook, so send a test to yourself first.

### Test it once

Send a test to yourself and open it on your phone and on your computer. Check that the layout, colours and gold button look right.

## Sending a quote

Work through these steps for every client. Type directly over the [square brackets]; the formatting stays.

1. Start a new message from your template (Gmail: three dots > Templates > Client quote; Apple Mail: paste from your saved draft).
2. Add the client's address and the subject: Your [cabin] options: [origin] to [destination]. For example, "Your Business options: London to New York".
3. In the heading, type the client's first name. Change "morning" to "afternoon" or "evening" to suit their time of day.
4. For each option, type the price over [USD 0,000]. Click into the dashed box, delete the bracketed text and paste the screenshot (in Gmail choose Best fit). On the line below, type the airline, the stops and anything worth noting.
5. Delete any option you do not need: select it from its label to the line under the screenshot, and press Delete.
6. Use the grey box for extra details about the flight plan, such as connections, baggage or how long the fares are held. Delete the box if you have nothing to add.
7. Type your name over [Advisor name] at the bottom.
8. Leave "To make a booking", "Good to know" and the Terms of Service box exactly as they are.
9. Go through the checklist below, then send.

### After the client replies

The client replies with a preferred option and, for every traveller, a first name, last name, date of birth and contact phone number. Check that each name matches the passport, then arrange the invoice for the booking.

Replies reach you in two ways. A client who presses Reply writes to your inbox; a client who clicks the gold button writes to quote@flyfirstclass.com. Watch both.

## Before you press send

Check each of these. A quote with a wrong price or a leftover bracket reaches the client as it stands.

- [ ] The client's address and the subject line are correct.
- [ ] The greeting has the client's first name, with morning, afternoon or evening to match.
- [ ] Each screenshot shows the right route, dates and cabin, and the price above it matches.
- [ ] No [square brackets] are left, including the signature at the bottom.
- [ ] Unused options and empty dashed boxes are deleted.
- [ ] "To make a booking", "Good to know" and the Terms of Service box are untouched.
- [ ] First time only: you sent yourself a test and checked it on your phone.

## If something looks wrong

Most problems have a quick fix. If yours is not here, send the message to yourself and show the office a screenshot.

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| A screenshot is too big or too small | Resize it to fit inside the dashed box, about 540 pixels wide. In Gmail, click the image and choose Best fit. |
| The logo does not show | It loads from our website, so it appears once the new site is live. Some mail apps also ask the client to show images. |
| Text you pasted has the wrong font or colour | Paste as plain text: Ctrl+Shift+V on Windows, Option+Shift+Cmd+V on a Mac. Typing over the brackets avoids this. |
| The layout looks broken after pasting | Delete the message. Re-open `quote-email.html`, select everything again and paste into a new message. |
| The font differs from our website | Some mailboxes substitute Helvetica or Arial. This is normal and does not affect the layout. |
